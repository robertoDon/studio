# Playful illustration · A character with something to say

## What it is

Sticker-style illustration on a dark grid, soft 3D characters and floating emojis, mascot figures, or hand-drawn and classic illustration mixed with the interface.

## Visual signals

- One illustration style across the whole product (same line, same light, same palette).
- Illustration with a job: empty state, onboarding, error, report. Never a stray ornament next to a form.
- Illustration palette = brand palette + 1–2 supporting colors.
- 3D characters with soft materials (matte, clay), no realistic texture.
- The text beside it says what to do; the illustration only sets the tone.

## When to use / When not to

- **Use:** consumer apps, B2C SaaS, annual reports, kids, events, education.
- **Don't use:** luxury, serious finance, legal, serious health.
- Before adding a mascot, check the house rules (DESIGN.md, a style guide, the project's own design-system or brand skill): some products forbid mascots. The house rules win.

## How to apply by surface

- **App:** empty state and onboarding; never in the middle of a work flow.
- **Landing page:** the hero or the "how it works" section.
- **Report:** one illustration per chapter.

## Within the motion ceiling

A character that waves on entry is a **reveal** (≤ 700 ms). Endless floating is a **loop**: allowed, and it turns off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. A reaction to a tap is a **touch** (≤ 160 ms).

## Examples (Muzli)

- Algorithm Fatigue (manychat.com/report), Assistantly, Roiheads, Dave Holloway, Maxima Therapy, Amazonia. Sources: https://muz.li/blog/the-best-creative-websites-of-the-month-april-2026/ and https://muz.li/blog/the-best-creative-websites-of-the-month-march-2026/

## Pitfall

A literal illustration of the product name (a library stamp for an app called "Overdue") is an AI default according to Muzli (`anti-generic`). Look for the less obvious metaphor.

## Checklist

1. Only one illustration style in the product?
2. Does the illustration have a job (empty, error, onboarding)?
3. Does the house yardstick allow a mascot?
4. Is the metaphor something other than the literal name?
