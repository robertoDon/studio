## Conflict with the motion ceiling

Trends 1–4, 9 and 16 depend on long, cinematic motion. Under the default ceiling (touch ≤ 160 ms, reveal ≤ 700 ms, scene ≤ 1.2 s including delays, loops excluded), pinned scroll and long hero sequences have to become short reveals, or be treated as an ambient loop. Each skill says how.
